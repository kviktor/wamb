About ISBN Lookup

This feature connects to the OpenLibrary API to automatically retrieve book information. You can use either ISBN-10 or ISBN-13 format, with or without hyphens.

Example ISBNs:

  • ISBN-10: 0451524934
  • ISBN-13: 978-0451524935
  • With hyphens: 978-0-451-52493-5
